# Current hackathon requirements

Official event: [PayPal AI Hackathon](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/). The [official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) govern; this summary was checked during the October 4 research.

Submission deadline is November 12, 2026 at noon PST, equivalent to November 13 at 1:30 a.m. IST. Multiple substantially different entries are permitted, subject to organizer judgment.

Each submission needs a functioning application combining PayPal and AI, a public GitHub repository with an open-source license and setup instructions, and a public YouTube demonstration under three minutes. Keep the working project available to judges through judging. Judges may assess the video without running the application.

## Judging

Five equally weighted criteria apply: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea and Presentation. The plans map each product's evidence to these criteria. Named honorable mentions such as Best Use of PayPal + AI and Best Use of Agentic Commerce are prize opportunities, not mandatory product tracks.

## Our build constraints

- PayPal sandbox only, using simulated funds. Zero PayPal account spending.
- Gemini is selected. The user authorized $10 total using existing API credits; persistent budget enforcement is active. No credit purchases or paid overages.
- Use original or appropriately licensed fixtures and assets. No dependence on private Shopify or Capital IQ data.
- Human approval and deterministic controls govern money and sensitive changes. Treat unknown outcomes as unknown.
- Show a clear customer problem within the first 5–10 seconds of each demo and real end-to-end evidence afterward.
- Test AI against strong practical alternatives. A protocol connection alone does not establish novelty or AI value.
- Build three independently useful applications; shared helpers must not turn them into renamed copies.
- Implementation is authorized. Public publishing, submission, paid hosting and live payment activity are not part of the current authorization.
